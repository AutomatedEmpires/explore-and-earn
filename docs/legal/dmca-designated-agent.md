# DMCA designated agent

Draft for counsel review, not legal advice. This is the filing sheet for the designated agent in the Terms (§11.1 item 14). It is not a completed designation. The founder files it with the United States Copyright Office. A designation is not effective until it appears in the directory at https://www.copyright.gov/dmca-directory/.

Counsel confirms the current form before anyone submits it. The fields below are the ones 37 CFR § 201.38 requires the Office's directory to collect: the service provider's full legal name, its alternate names, and its physical street address, plus the agent's name, mail address, telephone, and email. Values in brackets are not known to this draft.

## After filing

1. Submit through the Office's electronic system linked from https://www.copyright.gov/dmca-directory/.
2. Pay the fee the form shows that day. This draft does not state the fee.
3. Paste the public directory URL into the Terms once the listing is live.
4. Amend the designation when a name, address, phone, or email changes. An outdated listing does not meet the statute.
5. Keep the same contact on copyright notices sent to Explore&Earn. Do not publish a second, different agent in the Terms.

The Terms already tell a complainant what a § 512(c)(3) notice must contain, and they point at this agent. Until the directory listing exists, notices go to the contact in Terms section 18 with the subject "Copyright notice".
