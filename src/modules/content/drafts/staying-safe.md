## Before you apply (§7)

A job that includes housing, far from home, is worth a slower look.

- never pay to get a job. Explore&Earn does not charge seekers. A real employer will not ask for bank details before hiring, and will not ask for a deposit, a uniform fee, a training fee, or a visa fee in a message.
- Please check the employer's own website, and the careers contact that business publishes, before you travel.
- Please tell someone where you are going, who you are meeting, and when you expect to be back.

## What we check, and what we do not (§7)

We run business checks before a host's first listing is public, and we scan host messages for payment requests. Report and Block are on listings and in messages. A report of fraud or of danger to a person is reviewed within 4 hours in staffed hours and within 24 hours otherwise (legal review §7 item 5).

We never call a host verified, trusted, or safe. A badge that reads "Active Explore&Earn host since [date]" means a paid plan. It does not mean we verified the business, the housing, or the job. We may say what was checked and when. We do not say the result was "verified", "trusted", or "safe" (legal review §7 item 9).

## If something is wrong (§7)

Use Report. If you are being forced to work or cannot leave, call the National Human Trafficking Hotline at 1-888-373-7888 or text 233733. That path is legal review §7 item 7. If a host we confirm as fake already received your application or your messages, we write to you about what was exposed and what to do, with links to ReportFraud.ftc.gov and IdentityTheft.gov (legal review §7 item 6).
