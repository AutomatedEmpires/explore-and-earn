We don't give visa advice. This page points to the official US government pages for the programs that seasonal workers from abroad most often use. Rules change, and only the US government and your sponsor or employer can tell you what applies to you.

## J-1 Summer Work Travel

The J-1 Summer Work Travel program is for university students from abroad. You take part only through a sponsor that the US Department of State designates, and the sponsor vets the host employer (22 CFR 62.32). Start with the State Department's program page:

- [Summer Work Travel, US Department of State](https://j1visa.state.gov/programs/summer-work-travel/)

## H-2A: temporary agricultural work

H-2A lets US employers hire workers from abroad for temporary or seasonal farm work. The employer applies; a worker cannot apply on their own. An H-2A employer must provide housing at no cost to H-2A workers who cannot reasonably return home each day (20 CFR 655.122(d)(1)).

- [H-2A Temporary Agricultural Workers, US Citizenship and Immigration Services](https://www.uscis.gov/working-in-the-united-states/temporary-workers/h-2a-temporary-agricultural-workers)
- [H-2A program, US Department of Labor](https://www.dol.gov/agencies/eta/foreign-labor/programs/h-2a)

## H-2B: temporary non-agricultural work

H-2B lets US employers hire workers from abroad for temporary non-farm work, such as at resorts, lodges and on boats. The employer applies, and must first try to hire US workers. An employer may not charge you any fee for recruitment or for the visa (20 CFR 655.20(o)).

- [H-2B Temporary Non-Agricultural Workers, US Citizenship and Immigration Services](https://www.uscis.gov/working-in-the-united-states/temporary-workers/h-2b-temporary-non-agricultural-workers)
- [H-2B program, US Department of Labor](https://www.dol.gov/agencies/eta/foreign-labor/programs/h-2b)
- [SeasonalJobs.dol.gov, US Department of Labor](https://seasonaljobs.dol.gov/)

## What Explore&Earn does

A listing on Explore&Earn is not one of the recruitment steps the Department of Labor requires, and it does not replace any of them (legal review §2.3). Hosts see your work authorization only as "authorized" or "needs sponsorship". They never see a visa type or an end date. No host may charge you a fee to apply, to be hired, or for a visa.
