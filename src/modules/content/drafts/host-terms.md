Draft for counsel review, not legal advice. This draft stays a draft until a lawyer signs it. It is not published. Host Terms are in addition to the Terms of Service. Citations are to the November 2026 legal review unless a spec is named.

## 1. Authority (§11.1 host item 1)

The person who accepts these Host Terms must have authority to bind the organization. The organization is who we charge. Roles are Owner, Admin, Recruiter, and Billing. Seats follow the plan. A Recruiter sees only the listings in their scope. Billing cannot see applicants, candidates, or messages.

## 2. Plans, prices, and the founding offer (§11.1 host item 2)

Prices in the product design (section 16), which this draft states for counsel:

| Plan | Monthly | Annual (10× monthly) | Extra listing slot per month |
|---|---|---|---|
| Starter | $99 | $990 | $39 |
| Professional | $299 | $2,990 | $29 |
| Enterprise | $599 | $5,990 | $19 |

Plans renew until you cancel. You can cancel online in as few steps as sign-up. Before the card is charged we show the price, the renewal date, and how to cancel. We email a reminder before an annual renewal. A pause or a season pass, if offered, states what is charged while paused (legal review §8.3).

The first 100 host organizations are the only founding cohort. Two offer texts exist, and counsel must pick the one these terms publish:

- The text SP8 spec 8.5 still tells the Host Terms draft to carry: 90 days free from public launch, then 30% off for life, and that rate kept on a return within 6 months.
- Founder decision FD-7 (2026-11-04) withdrew that offer. The replacement is 50% off for six monthly terms, or 50% off the first annual term, for the first 100 organizations. Legal review §8.2 adds a 30-day publish condition, shown before the host relies on the offer. Hosts who joined under the earlier wording keep those terms, or get 30 days' notice of the new condition.

This draft does not choose between them. Taxes are extra where the law requires them. A subscription disclosure is shown before payment (legal review §8.3; P0 item 24).

Boosts and announcement packs, when those products are sold, are non-refundable once started and refunded in full if they cannot start (SP6 open item O4). Counsel confirms that sentence before it is published.

## 3. Listings (§11.1 host item 3)

A listing must be accurate and current, and it must be one real job. Pay is the good-faith range you expect to pay (legal review §2.1). Housing, meals, deposits, and deductions are stated truthfully and lawfully (legal review §2.4). We may pause a listing after two unanswered "Still hiring?" reminders, and we may remove a listing that breaks these terms. The pause warning names the date (legal review §8.3).

## 4. Your legal duties (§11.1 host item 4)

Compliance is the host's: wage and hour law, pay transparency, anti-discrimination, immigration and visa-program rules, child labor, housing standards, and whether a worker is an employee or a contractor. A listing on Explore&Earn does not replace an H-2B recruitment step (legal review §2.3). You are responsible for lawful listings and lawful hiring (legal review §2.2 item 6).

Hosts see a seeker's work authorization only as "authorized" or "needs sponsorship". You do not receive a visa type or an end date, and you will not ask for one through the product (legal review §2.2 item 4).

## 5. No fees from seekers (§11.1 host item 5)

You will not charge a seeker a fee or a deposit to apply, interview, or be hired, or for housing before an offer. You will charge no recruitment or visa fees at all (legal review §2.3). A housing deposit that is real belongs in the listing's housing section, not only in a message (legal review §7).

## 6. Applicant data (§11.1 host item 6)

Use an application only to consider that person for that job. No resale and no marketing. Keep it reasonably secure. Delete it when the seeker asks, or when your organization leaves Explore&Earn. Tell us of any breach of applicant data within 72 hours (legal review §4.4 and §4.7).

## 7. The candidate pool and invites (§11.1 host item 7)

Use the pool and invites within the product's fair-use limits. There is no bulk messaging of people who have not applied. Do not contact a seeker off the platform before the seeker shares contact details. You may not filter or sort candidates or applicants by age, date of birth, sex, photo, name, national origin, or visa type (legal review §2.2 item 3).

## 8. "As described" (§11.1 host item 8)

Workers may say whether the job was as described. You may reply. You may not edit an answer. You may not retaliate, pressure, or reward an answer (legal review §5). You may not hide one. We remove an answer only for a neutral written reason: fraud, abuse, or the wrong employer.

## 9. Operator-assisted listings (§11.1 host item 9)

If an operator helps write a listing, you consent in writing first. The operator saves a draft only. The host publishes, after reviewing every section, with the same attestations as any host (legal review §8.1). You are responsible for the facts. We do not invent pay, benefits, housing, or dates you did not supply.

## 10. Verification and the badge (§11.1 host item 10)

We may check that the sign-up email domain matches the business website, that the website and the business registration predate the account, and that the payment name and country fit the business (legal review §7). Passing those checks is not a promise that the business is safe. The subscriber badge means a paid plan only. Its tooltip reads "Active Explore&Earn host since [date]" (FD-4; legal review §5). It does not mean verified, trusted, or safe.

## 11. Suspension (§11.1 host item 11)

Suspension or termination may follow a breach of these terms, fraud, or a legal duty. Listings come down. Applicant data is handled as section 6 and the Privacy Policy describe. Fees already earned are handled under the refund rule counsel approves in section 2.

## 12. Indemnity (§11.1 host item 12)

Indemnity: the organization will defend Explore&Earn against a claim that arises from the organization's listings, hiring decisions, housing, or misuse of applicant data, and will pay the resulting damages and reasonable legal costs, to the extent the law allows. Our liability cap in the Terms of Service also applies to hosts, to the extent the law allows (legal review §13 question 12).
