Draft for counsel review, not legal advice. This draft stays a draft until a lawyer signs it. It is not published. Citations are to the November 2026 legal review unless a spec is named. Square brackets are facts the founder still has to supply.

## 1. The contract (§11.1 item 1)

These Terms of Service are a contract between you and Explore&Earn. They cover seekers and everyone else who uses the service. Hosts also accept the Host Terms. We may change these terms. A material change takes effect no sooner than 30 days after we schedule it, and we send the notice the product already defines for a material legal update (SP8 spec 8.5). The first published version takes effect when it is published. If you keep using Explore&Earn after the effective date, you are agreeing to the new terms. If you do not agree, stop using the service and close your account.

## 2. What Explore&Earn is (§11.1 item 2)

Explore&Earn is a venue where employers post jobs and people apply. We are not the employer, not a recruiter, not an employment agency, and not a party to any job, housing arrangement, or hire. We do not interview, select, refer, or place anyone. Hosts make every hiring decision (legal review §2.7). We do not guarantee a job, an employer, a listing's accuracy, housing, meals, hours, or a hire.

## 3. Who may use it (§11.1 item 3)

You must be 18 or older. One person, one account, one role (founder decision FD-1). The information you give us must be accurate. You may not open an account for someone else. Listings are for paid work only (product design section 19).

## 4. Seekers are free (§11.1 item 4)

Free for seekers. Explore&Earn does not charge a seeker to create a profile, apply, interview, be hired, or be housed. Nobody else may charge a seeker to apply, interview, be hired, or be housed before an offer (legal review §2.3 and §7). A host may not collect a recruitment fee or a visa fee at all.

Work authorization, when a seeker chooses to provide it, is shown to hosts as two answers only: "authorized" or "needs sponsorship". Hosts do not see a visa type or an end date (legal review §2.2 item 4; founder decision of 2026-11-12, explore-and-earn#45 comment 6836991077).

## 5. Your content (§11.1 item 5)

You keep ownership of the text and photos you post. In short: you keep ownership, and you license us to host and show the content. You give Explore&Earn a license to host, store, and show that content so the service can run, including to the hosts you apply to or agree to be found by. You confirm you have the right to post it. Photos must not show an identifiable child (legal review §2.6 and §3).

## 6. Rules of use (§11.1 item 6)

Do not use Explore&Earn for fraud, discrimination, harassment, scraping, an off-platform payment request, or unlawful content. The Community Guidelines are part of these terms. We may remove content or close an account that breaks them. Block and Report are available on listings, profiles, and messages (legal review §7).

## 7. Safety and "As described" (§11.1 item 7)

We check some things and not others. Business verification runs before a host's first listing is public. We do not promise that a host, a listing, housing, or a workplace is safe. "As described" answers are the worker's own opinions about housing, meals, and pay. They are not our findings. How to report a problem is on the listing and in the first message of a thread. See the Staying safe page (legal review §7).

## 8. Child safety (§11.1 item 8)

Uploads are scanned. If we obtain actual knowledge of apparent child sexual abuse material, we report it to NCMEC and keep the material as 18 U.S.C. § 2258A requires (legal review §3.1). We do not email that material, paste it into a ticket, or send it to an AI model.

## 9. Non-consensual intimate images (§11.1 item 9)

Non-consensual intimate images: you may ask us to remove an image of you that was posted without your consent. Write to the contact in section 18 and say where the image appears. How fast we must act, and whether the TAKE IT DOWN Act covers Explore&Earn at launch, is legal review §13 question 10. Until a lawyer answers, this section describes the request path and does not promise a deadline.

## 10. Pay estimates (§11.1 item 10)

An after-housing estimate, if we show one, is built only from what the host entered: pay, weekly hours, and housing, meal, and other amounts marked as deducted from pay. It is before taxes. It is not an offer and not a guarantee of hours or pay (legal review §9). The host's own pay range stays on the listing with equal or greater prominence. We do not call the estimate "take-home pay".

## 11. The fit score (§11.1 item 11)

A fit score, when one is shown, ranks a candidate pool or sorts applicants. It uses job-match facts the seeker provided, such as occupation, availability, skills, certifications, the two work-authorization answers, pay preference, and location preference. On a remote listing it may also use the seeker's state as work-location eligibility; counsel has not signed that use (SP5 open item 2). It never uses a protected trait, a photo, a name, age, or gender. It does not decide who is interviewed, hired, or rejected (legal review §2.8). A person at the host makes the hiring decision. You may ask for a human review of a score.

## 12. Messages (§11.1 item 12)

Messages are scanned for scams and safety before a host's pre-hire message is shown to a seeker (legal review §7). The scan looks for requests for money, bank details, and similar patterns. It does not mean we have read every conversation or that a conversation is safe.

## 13. Honest answers (§11.1 item 13)

Honest answers about a job are welcome, and nothing in these terms limits your right to give an honest review or an honest "As described" answer, or penalizes you for one (legal review §5; 15 U.S.C. § 45b).

## 14. Copyright (§11.1 item 14)

If you believe content on Explore&Earn infringes a copyright, send a notice that meets 17 U.S.C. § 512(c)(3) to our designated agent. The agent's name and address are published in the Copyright Office directory once the founder files them, and on the page linked from these terms. Until that filing appears in the directory, use the contact in section 18 and say it is a copyright notice. See `docs/legal/dmca-designated-agent.md`.

## 15. Closing your account (§11.1 item 15)

You may close your account in the product. What we keep, and why, is in the Privacy Policy, including shared application records, safety records, billing records, legal holds, and child-safety evidence kept for the period the law requires (legal review §11.2 item 10 and §3.1).

## 16. Disclaimers and liability (§11.1 item 16)

The service is provided as available. To the extent the law allows, we disclaim warranties of accuracy, safety, fitness for a particular purpose, and non-infringement. To the extent the law allows, the limit of our liability for a claim arising out of the service is the amount you paid us in the three months before the claim, or one hundred dollars if you paid nothing. This limit does not apply where the law forbids it, including a liability that cannot be waived. Counsel decides whether this cap is enforceable (legal review §13 question 12).

## 17. Disputes (§11.1 item 17)

Before filing a claim, you and we will try informal resolution: a written notice that describes the dispute, sent to the contact in section 18, and thirty days to discuss it. After that, the dispute is heard in the courts of the place counsel names, under that place's law. These terms do not include an arbitration clause or a class waiver unless counsel adds one (legal review §13 question 12).
