## 1. What Community is

Community is where seekers share photos from the places they work and travel. It is for signed-in seekers only, and search engines do not index it. Hosts do not post photos; they appear only in clearly labeled paid announcements and boosted jobs (product design section 13).

## 2. Photos only

A post is one photo. There are no captions, tags, places or feelings, and there are no comments anywhere. Each photo has a short description for people who use screen readers; we suggest one, and you can edit it. You can react to a photo, once.

## 3. What you may not post

Do not post:

- sexual content or nudity, or any image of a child that is sexual or exploitative;
- an image of an identifiable child;
- violence, gore, or threats;
- hate, harassment, or content that targets someone for who they are;
- an intimate image of someone without their consent;
- someone else's private information, such as an address, a phone number, or a document;
- a photo you don't have the right to share;
- spam, advertising, a job offer, or a request for money;
- anything illegal, or anything that breaks the Terms of Service.

A description must describe the photo. It is checked like the photo is.

## 4. Child safety

Every image uploaded to Explore&Earn is checked against known child sexual abuse material by hash matching (Microsoft PhotoDNA) before anyone else can see it. A match is blocked and preserved, and we report it to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) as federal law requires (18 U.S.C. § 2258A; legal review §3). We never show, email or forward that material, except to NCMEC as the law requires.

## 5. Reporting and blocking

Block and Report are on every photo and profile. Use "Report" for anything that breaks these guidelines. If a photo is an intimate image of you shared without your consent, report it with that reason and we will remove it and copies of it (legal review §3.4).

## 6. What happens when something breaks the rules

Photos are checked automatically before they are posted, and a person reviews anything uncertain. We may remove a photo, hide it while we review it, restrict you from posting for a time, or close your account. When we remove a photo or restrict you, we tell you why, using a fixed reason, and you can appeal through the Help center. A removed photo is deleted 30 days after removal. A restriction is kept for 12 months after it ends.
