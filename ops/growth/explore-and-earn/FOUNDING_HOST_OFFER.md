# Explore&Earn Founding Host Offer

Last updated: 2026-07-12
Status: outreach-ready copy based on repository pricing canon; acceptance and billing still follow the product's normal host flow

## External-facing offer

### Help build the place-based work marketplace you wish already existed

Explore&Earn is a seeker-first discovery marketplace for farms, ranches, maritime operators, lodges, resorts, camps, outdoor businesses, and other place-based employers. Every opportunity answers three questions before a seeker applies:

- **Housing:** Where will I sleep?
- **Meals:** What will I eat?
- **Pay:** What will I earn?

The Founding Host Program is limited to 100 paid host seats that help shape how that experience works in practice. You keep control of your company profile, your opportunity details, and the original media you choose to upload. Explore&Earn does not copy a listing from somewhere else or represent your company without approval.

### What Founding Hosts receive

- a lifetime-locked Founding Host subscription price while the qualifying subscription remains active;
- guided setup for the company profile and first original opportunity;
- a structured Housing / Meals / Pay readiness review before submission;
- a direct channel for product feedback during the founding cohort;
- cohort recognition as a Founding Host, separate from any identity or claim-verification representation; and
- access to the plan entitlements included with the selected tier.

### Why Explore&Earn is different

Explore&Earn is not positioned as another general job board. It focuses on Farm, Maritime, Remote, and Seasonal work and organizes seeker discovery around one practical decision card:

- **Seek** for intentional category and fit search;
- **Swipe** for quick, mobile exploration;
- **Map** for place-based discovery; and
- **Housing / Meals / Pay** visible before application.

Established platforms may be stronger on inventory volume, category depth, professional credentials, remote breadth, or work-exchange community. Explore&Earn's founding-host test is whether a host-controlled, more visual and structured opportunity can make the actual arrangement clearer and reduce avoidable expectation mismatches. This is a hypothesis to measure, not a promise of applicant volume, quality, placement, or reduced workload.

The host creates and approves the information. Evidence photos must be uploaded by the host with the right to use them and should support the current employee arrangement—not reuse guest marketing images or third-party screenshots. `Host-provided`, `variable`, `unknown`, and `last confirmed` qualifiers must stay visible; evidence is not an independent inspection or legal certification.

“Lifetime-locked” is host-seat scoped, follows the host through plan changes, and is permanently forfeited if the subscription is cancelled. The program has a hard cap of 100 paid seats. Any remaining-seat count must come from live subscription data and must never be guessed.

## What participation requires

The host agrees to:

1. authorize Explore&Earn to display the company profile and host-created opportunity;
2. provide accurate, current, original information rather than pasting a third-party listing;
3. answer Housing, Meals, and Pay, including “not provided” where that is the honest answer;
4. upload only media the host owns or has the right to use, with no screenshots or copied images from another site;
5. distinguish estimates, variable arrangements, and seasonal ranges from guaranteed terms;
6. keep a reachable business contact and respond to applicant questions in a reasonable period;
7. follow applicable employment, wage, housing, safety, nondiscrimination, and privacy obligations; and
8. accept platform review, correction requests, reporting, and removal processes.

The host should also identify whether each opportunity is paid employment, exchange, volunteer service, internship/education, licensed or credentialed maritime work, shore-side work, or another legally distinct arrangement. Explore&Earn must not blur those categories for a simpler pitch.

## Honest trust language

Use:

> “Founding Host” means this host joined the limited founding paid cohort and contributed early product feedback.

Do not use:

> “Explore&Earn has verified this company, its housing, its pay, or its employment practices.”

The product currently describes a “Verified Host” badge as attached to an active paid subscription and discloses that it is not independent verification of identity, property, or listing claims. Never imply otherwise. Trust and safety decisions, badge issuance rules, KYC, background screening, and legal policy require founder approval.

Until that drift is resolved by the founder, acquisition messages must not promise or market a Verified Host badge. “Founding Host” may be used only for an accepted seat in the limited paid cohort.

We are inviting a paid founding cohort capped at 100 host seats to shape the workflow and lock in Founding Host pricing while the qualifying subscription stays active. We do not copy your jobs from another site—you create and control the profile, opportunity, and media. Would a 15-minute fit conversation be useful for your next hiring cycle?

## Operator qualification checklist

Before presenting the offer, confirm:

- the person is authorized to discuss recruiting or operations;
- a recurring or upcoming opportunity may exist, without assuming that it does;
- the host is willing to answer the triad honestly;
- there is no unresolved request to stop contact;
- pricing is quoted exactly from the current product source of truth; and
- no unapproved promise has been made about applicant volume, placement, ranking, verification, vetting, trial length, refunds, or launch date.

## Acceptance path

1. Host agrees to a fit call.
2. Operator sends the official host onboarding link and program terms.
3. Host chooses a plan in the product; the operator does not collect payment details by email or phone.
4. Host creates its own profile and opportunity.
5. Explore&Earn reviews completeness and trust/safety flags; review is not a legal or factual certification.
6. Host corrects any gaps and submits for the normal publication workflow.

An outreach reply is not acceptance, a contact-form response is not a partnership, and a draft profile is not a live listing.
