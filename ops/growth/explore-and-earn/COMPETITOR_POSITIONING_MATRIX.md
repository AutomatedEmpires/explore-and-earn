## Comparison rules for external copy

Use:

- “Explore&Earn can be clearer on…”
- “Explore&Earn should differentiate by…”
- “The opportunity is…”
- “Do not compete head-on here; use this as discovery/source intelligence…”
- “Competitor appears stronger on…”

Do not say:

- “We are better than [platform].”
- “[Platform] is unsafe/unverified/outdated.”
- “[Platform] hides pay/housing/meals,” unless a current official source supports the exact scope.
- “Explore&Earn verifies jobs/housing/pay” without a precise approved qualifier and implemented process.
