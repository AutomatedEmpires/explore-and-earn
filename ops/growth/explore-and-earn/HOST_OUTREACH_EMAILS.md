## Objection handling

### “We already hire elsewhere.”

> That makes sense, and Explore&Earn does not need to replace a channel that works. The question is whether a host-controlled Discovery Card with Housing, Meals, Pay, place, and evidence upfront could reduce repetitive questions or expectation gaps for seekers who choose by location and living setup. Where does your current channel already do this well, and where do candidates still need clarification? If there is no gap, I’ll close the loop.

### “Why should we maintain another listing?”

> The first test has to earn the work it creates. We would guide one original host profile and one opportunity, reuse company-level details, and keep variable or unknown fields explicit. We will not import or mirror a listing from another platform. What would need to be reusable or connected to your current process for a pilot to be reasonable?

### “Are you better than [platform]?”

> That platform may be stronger at its own job—volume, category depth, professional credentials, remote screening, or exchange community. Explore&Earn should earn a place by making the practical arrangement and adventure context clearer. I’d rather understand what already works for you and whether this fills a real gap.

### “Housing varies.”

> Variation is useful information. The listing can state what is provided, partial, not provided, variable, or still to be confirmed, and can separate shared/private options, cost, availability, and evidence. We would never turn a changing arrangement into a blanket promise.

### “We are not ready.”

> Understood. We can record the month you expect to plan the next season and stop the current sequence. No placeholder listing or partnership claim will be created in the meantime. What month, if any, should we revisit?

### “What does this cost?”

> The Founding Host rates currently in the product are Starter at $149/month or $1,490/year, Professional at $299/month or $2,990/year, and Enterprise at $599/month or $5,990/year. Annual is presented as two months free. The rate stays locked while the qualifying subscription remains active and is forfeited on cancellation. I can send the plan entitlements; I cannot promise an unapproved trial, waiver, refund, or custom discount.

### “How are applicants vetted?”

> Explore&Earn provides profiles, applications, messaging, reporting, and host review tools; it does not promise that every applicant has been background-checked or independently verified. You remain responsible for your lawful hiring and screening process. We’ll state the actual screening scope clearly rather than use “vetted” as a vague assurance.
