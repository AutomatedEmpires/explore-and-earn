# Explore&Earn Founding Host Pitch V2

Last updated: 2026-07-12

Use: founder and operator talk track for approved one-to-one conversations. This is not a send-ready mass-email asset and does not override the postal-address, suppression, pricing, legal, trust/safety, or launch gates.

## Core pitch

> Explore&Earn is a seeker-first marketplace for Farm, Maritime, Remote, and Seasonal work. Instead of asking a traveler to piece the decision together across a job description, search results, and follow-up emails, every host-created Discovery Card answers three practical questions: Where will I sleep? What will I eat? What will I earn?
>
> Seekers discover opportunities through Seek, Swipe, and Map; hosts control their profile, opportunity details, and original evidence. We do not scrape your listing from another platform, copy your photos, or represent your company before you opt in.
>
> We are inviting a paid founding cohort, capped at 100 host seats, to shape the workflow. The hypothesis is simple: when place, living setup, and compensation are clearer before application, both sides can spend more time on genuine fit and less on avoidable expectation gaps. We cannot promise applicant volume or placement, but we can build and measure the first host-controlled profile with you.

## 30-second version

> Explore&Earn helps adventure and place-based employers show applicants the practical arrangement before they apply: Housing, Meals, and Pay, inside a visual Discovery Card across Seek, Swipe, and Map. Hosts create and control the information and evidence; we never copy a job from another board. We are inviting a paid founding cohort to shape the workflow and test whether clearer expectations improve applicant fit. Would a 15-minute discovery call be useful for your next season?

## Why Explore&Earn is different

- **Focused, not generic:** four lanes—Farm, Maritime, Remote, and Seasonal—rather than every occupation.
- **Seeker-first:** seekers remain free; discovery starts with the place and practical arrangement, not a long list of keywords.
- **One decision primitive:** the Discovery Card keeps Housing, Meals, and Pay visible instead of reducing them to a generic perks paragraph.
- **Natural discovery modes:** Seek for intentional search, Swipe for quick exploration, and Map for place-based comparison.
- **Host-controlled evidence:** hosts upload only information and photos they own or have rights to use, with dates and variable/unknown states.
- **Trust through precision:** a host profile, clear qualifiers, and evidence states explain what is known without pretending Explore&Earn independently inspected a property or guaranteed a claim.
- **Modern application context:** seeker profiles and applications should help a host understand fit without forcing applicants to reconstruct the same lifestyle and availability details repeatedly.

These are design and positioning commitments. Operators must distinguish the current product from planned or future capabilities and must not promise a launch date or unapproved feature.

## Competitive framing

Use only when a host brings up another platform or asks directly.

> Established platforms already do important things well: broad aggregators deliver scale, seasonal and category boards concentrate relevant jobs, remote platforms build search and trust signals, and work-exchange communities help people understand hosted experiences. Explore&Earn is not trying to erase those strengths.
>
> The opening we are testing is pre-application decision clarity for an adventure-oriented seeker. Can one host-controlled card show the place, the arrangement, Housing, Meals, Pay, and supporting evidence clearly enough that the applicant understands what they are considering? A founding host helps us test that alongside—not necessarily instead of—the channels already in use.

Never say a competitor is unsafe, unverified, outdated, text-heavy, employer-first, or missing a feature unless the current public evidence supports the narrow statement and the source is retained.

## Why participate early

Founding Hosts receive:

- a lifetime-locked Founding Host subscription price while the qualifying subscription stays active;
- guided setup for the company profile and first original opportunity;
- a structured Housing / Meals / Pay readiness review;
- a direct product-feedback channel during the founding cohort;
- cohort recognition as a Founding Host, distinct from any verification claim; and
- the entitlements of the selected paid plan.

Founding participation is useful when the host wants to influence:

- how variable housing and meal arrangements are represented;
- what evidence is practical and safe to show;
- which pay assumptions need dates, ranges, or explanations;
- how seeker profiles and applications should complement an ATS or email flow;
- which credentials and eligibility fields are category-specific; and
- how much profile information can be reused across roles and seasons.

## What the host controls

The host:

- authorizes the company profile and each opportunity;
- creates or approves the original role information;
- answers Housing / Meals / Pay honestly, including `not provided`, `variable`, `unknown`, or `confirm by [date]`;
- uploads only rights-cleared media and evidence;
- identifies arrangement type, location, season, work authorization, eligibility, credentials, and screening process;
- updates or removes stale information; and
- remains responsible for lawful recruiting, screening, employment, housing, compensation, safety, privacy, and nondiscrimination.

Explore&Earn does not copy third-party inventory, certify employment law compliance, inspect housing, guarantee pay, perform unspecified background checks, or promise applicant outcomes.

## Category variants

### Farm

> For farm and ranch opportunities, we want the arrangement to be unmistakable: paid employment, internship, education, volunteer service, or exchange. Then the card shows the real Housing / Meals / Pay terms and what varies by role or season. How do you explain that today?

### Maritime

> Maritime work has real category boundaries. Explore&Earn should not blur licensed vessel roles, unlicensed crew, fisheries, shore-side marina work, marine trades, and waterfront hospitality. A founding maritime host can help us make credentials and the on-site arrangement clear without pretending to replace a professional credential board.

### Remote

> Explore&Earn is not pursuing every remote office role. The Remote lane connects flexible work to travel, outdoor, seasonal, farm, or maritime life, with hiring geography, timezone, travel, schedule, and Pay clear. Housing and Meals can be explicitly not provided rather than silently omitted.

### Seasonal

> Seasonal work is where place and logistics can determine whether a role is viable. We want a seeker to see season dates, access, Housing, Meals, Pay, and current evidence in the same decision flow. Which of those details creates the most repeated questions for your team?

## Discovery questions

Choose four or five:

1. Which channels currently produce the best-fit applicants, and what do they do well?
2. What do candidates still ask before applying or accepting?
3. Where do Housing, Meals, and Pay live today, and who keeps them current?
4. Which facts vary by role, property, season, or date?
5. What could you show as evidence without exposing private employee spaces or sensitive addresses?
6. Would candidates understand the opportunity better through search, a map, a quick card, or a detailed page—and why?
7. What must remain in your ATS or existing application process?
8. What category-specific credentials, age, work authorization, or safety requirements must be impossible to miss?
9. What would make another host profile too much work to maintain?
10. What result would make a one-opportunity pilot worth continuing?

Do not ask for applicant personal data, confidential employee information, unpublished compensation, or details the contact is not authorized to share.

## Objection handling

### “We already hire elsewhere.”

> Keep using what works. Explore&Earn is testing a complementary decision layer for seekers who choose by place and living setup. If Housing, Meals, Pay, and evidence were visible before application, would that reduce any repeated questions or expectation gaps? If not, we should not add another channel.

### “Housing varies.”

> Then `variable` is the accurate answer. The host can show which options exist, cost or deductions, dates, availability, and what still needs confirmation. We would rather preserve the variation than turn it into a blanket promise.

### “We are not ready.”

> Understood. We will not create a placeholder listing or imply a relationship. We can close the sequence or record the month your planning begins, whichever you prefer.

### “What does this cost?”

> Founding pricing is Starter at $149 monthly or $1,490 annually, Professional at $299 or $2,990, and Enterprise at $599 or $5,990. Annual is two months free. The price stays locked while the qualifying subscription remains active and is forfeited on cancellation. I can send the exact plan entitlements; I cannot invent a trial, refund, or custom discount.

### “How are applicants vetted?”

> We should be precise rather than use `vetted` as a blanket promise. Explore&Earn provides the currently approved profile, application, messaging, reporting, and host-review tools; it does not promise every applicant has an independent identity or background check. Your team remains responsible for lawful screening and hiring, and we will state the actual platform check scope clearly.

### “Can you guarantee better applicants?”

> No. The hypothesis is that clearer expectations can reduce avoidable mismatches, but only a measured pilot can tell us whether that is true for your operation. We would agree on a small test and review the questions, applications, corrections, and host workload.

### “Are you better than [platform]?”

> That platform may be stronger at its own job—volume, category depth, professional credentials, remote screening, or exchange community. Explore&Earn should earn a place by being clearer about the practical arrangement and the adventure context. I would rather understand where your current channel works and whether this fills a real gap.

## Close

> Based on what you described, the useful test would be one host-created opportunity for [season/role], with [two variable details] represented explicitly and [evidence type] controlled by your team. The next step is a 15-minute workflow review and the official Founding Host terms. Would [time A] or [time B] work?

If there is no clear test, close the loop. Do not manufacture urgency, scarcity, partnership, applicant demand, or a listing.

- “You are already featured/verified/partnered.”
- “We are better than CoolWorks/FlexJobs/Indeed/[competitor].”
- “Your housing/pay is verified.”
- “All applicants are vetted.”
- “We guarantee qualified applicants, hires, or less work.”
- “There are [number] seats left,” unless read from live approved data.
- “Text me back,” when the public number has not opted into SMS.
