# Explore&Earn Category Search Playbook

Last updated: 2026-07-12

Purpose: expand founding-host discovery across Farm, Maritime, Remote, and Seasonal without turning a competitor's listings into Explore&Earn inventory.

## The two-stage rule

Every search follows the same boundary:

1. **Market-intelligence stage:** public search results, competitor category pages, directories, and association indexes may reveal vocabulary, regions, business names, and recurring employer types.
2. **Official-source stage:** open the business's public official website and independently confirm its identity, operating model, location, and current public business contact route.

Only the official-source stage can create or refresh a CRM lead. A competitor URL may be retained in a private research note as the discovery path, but `website`, `source_url`, and `contact_page` in the host pipeline must cite the official host or an authoritative public directory under the existing source policy. Never carry a competitor's job description, photo, pay, housing, meals, dates, contact person, or application instructions into Explore&Earn.

## Universal operator workflow

| Step | Operator action | Required output | Stop condition |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Define lane | Select one category, region, and business type | Search batch name | Query is too broad to review manually |
| 2. Discover | Run a small set of public queries manually | Employer names and discovery URLs only | Login, CAPTCHA, robots restriction, or technical block |
| 3. Resolve identity | Find the business's own HTTPS domain | Official homepage | No reliable domain or conflicting identities |
| 4. Verify fit | Read public About, Contact, and Careers/Employment pages | Original one-sentence fit hypothesis | Business is staffing agency, duplicate, closed, or out of scope |
| 5. Verify contact | Record only a published business email, phone, named role, or contact form | Cited contact route | Guessed address, personal number, or hidden/login-only route |
| 6. Bound claims | Mark season, housing, meals, and pay as questions unless the official host page supports a limited statement | `unknown`, `inferred`, or cited official fact | Only third-party evidence exists |
| 7. Qualify | Apply lane criteria and portfolio needs | Add, reserve, or reject | No recurring need hypothesis or no safe route |
| 8. Pre-outreach refresh | Re-open source and contact pages; check suppression | Approval timestamp | Source stale, suppressed, postal-address gate incomplete |

The operator records observations in original language. A search result snippet is navigation help, not evidence.

## Farm

### Platforms and discovery sources

- RanchWork, RanchWorldAds, AgCareers, and Farm Job Search for employer-name and role-language intelligence only.
- WWOOF, Workaway, and Worldpackers for understanding work-exchange patterns and identifying business types; never infer a paid job from an exchange profile.
- Official farm, ranch, orchard, vineyard, farm-stay, agritourism, agricultural resort, and food-production career pages.
- Public state agriculture, extension, agritourism, commodity association, tourism, chamber, and farm-stay directories.

### Safe queries

```text
farm seasonal employment housing official
ranch jobs housing official careers
guest ranch employment official site
farm stay careers seasonal official
orchard harvest employment official contact
agritourism seasonal staff official careers
site:.gov agritourism directory [state]
site:.org guest ranch directory [state]
```

### Official-source follow-up

1. Search the exact business name in quotes with `official`, then confirm the domain from branding, address, and public business records or an authoritative directory.
2. Read the host's About/Operations page to distinguish a working farm, visitor attraction, lodging operation, staffing service, nonprofit, and informal exchange host.
3. Look for a public Employment/Careers page, then a Contact or Team page. Do not copy role text; record only whether an official recruiting route exists.
4. Ask whether the arrangement is paid employment, an internship, education, volunteer service, or lodging/food exchange. Do not collapse those legal relationships into one listing type.

### Exclusions

- Household-only or private-host exchange profiles without a public business identity.
- Labor contractors, recruiters, or staffing agencies presented as the end host.
- Volunteer or membership exchange treated as employment without host confirmation and legal review.
- Operations whose only evidence is a marketplace listing, social post, or cached snippet.
- Claims of housing, meals, acreage, organic certification, pay, tips, or season dates sourced only from a third party.

### Lead qualification

- Public business identity and official site.
- In-scope operation with agriculture plus recurring, visitor, lodging, food, or seasonal labor context.
- Safe public contact route controlled by the business.
- A decision-quality reason to clarify Housing / Meals / Pay.
- Willingness to distinguish paid work, exchange, education, and volunteer arrangements before publication.

### Verify before outreach

- legal/business name used publicly; operating location; current public contact route;
- whether recruiting or operations is the correct conversation;
- whether the season is official or an internal hypothesis;
- suppression status and commercial-email prerequisites; and
- that no competitor claim or exchange-profile detail appears in personalization.

## Maritime

### Platforms and discovery sources

- MaritimeJobs, Sea Career, gCaptain Jobs, Dockwalk, Crewseekers, and WorkBoat for occupational vocabulary, credential patterns, employer names, and sector intelligence only.
- Official marina, boatyard, shipyard, harbor, ferry, cruise, fishing, marine-tour, waterfront resort, and coastal hospitality career pages.
- Public marina associations, port authorities, harbor directories, state boating directories, tourism offices, chambers, and marine-trade associations.

### Safe queries

```text
marina seasonal jobs official careers housing
waterfront resort marina employment official
boatyard careers official [state]
shipyard careers entry level official [region]
ferry seasonal employment official
marine tour operator careers official
harbor seasonal staff official contact
site:.gov marina directory [state]
site:.org marina association member directory [state]
```

### Official-source follow-up

1. Resolve the vessel operator, marina, port, resort, or yard to its official domain; do not treat a recruiter or crewing intermediary as the host.
2. Determine whether the likely opportunity is professional/credentialed maritime, unlicensed vessel work, shore-side marine trade, marina operations, fisheries processing, or seasonal coastal hospitality.
3. On official pages, record only the existence of credential or eligibility requirements; the host must submit the actual requirements.
4. Keep licensed maritime pathways distinct from entry-level seasonal marina, dock, launch, guest-service, coastal-tourism, and resort roles.

### Exclusions

- Individual vessel/crew advertisements without a verifiable public business operator.
- Credential claims, vessel specifications, itineraries, rotations, contracts, or compensation copied from a maritime board.
- Roles whose legal jurisdiction, seafarer agreement, work authorization, or safety requirements cannot be identified by the host.
- Private phone numbers or messaging handles obtained from crew marketplaces.

### Lead qualification

- Official operating entity with a public business domain and contact route.
- Clear classification as professional maritime, shore-side marine, seasonal marina/coastal, or fisheries.
- Host can state required licenses, certificates, minimum age, work authorization, and safety expectations.
- For Explore&Earn's first wave, prefer seasonal/adventure maritime and shore-side operators; use professional boards as category intelligence rather than a volume battleground.

### Verify before outreach

- operator identity, worksite/vessel context, geography/jurisdiction, and business contact;
- whether licensing is likely and who is authorized to confirm it;
- that season, berth/housing, meals aboard, pay, schedule, and travel are still questions unless host-cited; and
- no cold SMS or off-platform personal contact.

## Remote

### Platforms and discovery sources

- FlexJobs, Remote OK, We Work Remotely, LinkedIn Jobs, Indeed, ZipRecruiter, Google job results, Glassdoor, and SimplyHired for category demand, titles, filters, and employer-name discovery only.
- Official career pages for travel, outdoor, conservation, tourism, hospitality, recreation, mapping, marine, agriculture, seasonal-program, and destination businesses that employ remote or hybrid teams.
- Public accelerator, association, tourism-tech, outdoor-industry, and remote-employer directories where business identity is visible without login.

### Safe queries

```text
remote seasonal customer support travel company careers
remote reservations outdoor travel official careers
remote guest experience adventure company careers
remote operations tourism company official jobs
remote marine technology careers official
remote agriculture technology careers official
remote conservation program careers official
site:company.com careers remote seasonal
```

### Official-source follow-up

1. Resolve the employer name to its official domain and open its careers page directly.
2. Verify whether `remote` means anywhere, country-limited, state-limited, hybrid, or temporarily remote. Do not import the board's filter label.
3. Qualify for adventure relevance: the company or role should connect to place, travel, outdoor, seasonal, farm, or maritime life—not merely be a generic office job that happens to be remote.
4. Treat timezone, residency, work authorization, equipment, travel, pay, and location requirements as host-owned fields.

### Exclusions

- Generic remote roles with no meaningful connection to Explore&Earn's category and lifestyle thesis.
- Employer identity hidden behind an agency, confidential listing, or login.
- Any listing copied from a subscription service or aggregator.
- Remote status, salary, benefits, or geography inferred from a stale search snippet.
- Multi-level marketing, fee-to-apply, training-fee, reshipping, or unverifiable opportunities.

### Lead qualification

- Official employer and career page, clear adventure/category relevance, and a public recruiting or business route.
- Remote arrangement can be represented precisely by geography, timezone, travel, and schedule.
- Host can publish the original opportunity and answer Pay; Housing and Meals can be explicitly `not provided` or `not applicable` rather than omitted.
- Prefer differentiated lifestyle context over breadth; Explore&Earn should not chase generic remote volume.

### Verify before outreach

- official remote policy or ask it as a question;
- hiring geography and work authorization;
- whether the role connects to a Farm, Maritime, Remote lifestyle, or Seasonal experience;
- current business contact and suppression status; and
- no implication that the competitor screened or endorsed the employer.

## Seasonal

### Platforms and discovery sources

- CoolWorks, Occupation Wild, Backdoorjobs, SeasonalJobs, SeasonWorkers, and relevant Alaska tourism job resources for season, region, and employer-name intelligence only.
- Official resort, lodge, camp, campground, national/state park concessioner, outfitter, guide, outdoor operator, seasonal program, and remote hospitality career pages.
- Public tourism, park concession, chamber, campground, resort, camp, ski-area, guide/outfitter, lodging, and destination directories.

### Safe queries

```text
seasonal resort jobs housing official careers
summer camp employment housing meals official
remote lodge seasonal employment official
campground seasonal staff official careers
ski resort seasonal employment official housing
outfitter guide jobs official careers [state]
seasonal hospitality jobs official [region]
site:.gov tourism directory resort [state]
site:.org campground association directory [state]
```

### Official-source follow-up

1. Confirm the employer's official domain and operation type.
2. Find its own Careers/Employment page and then the public Contact page; do not paste the competitor listing into search or CRM notes.
3. Separate the operating season from the employment term. Guest dates may support a timing hypothesis but do not prove hiring dates.
4. Mark employee housing, meals, deductions, transport, uniform, pass, and pay as unknown until the host submits or confirms them from an authorized source.

### Exclusions

- A board profile or listing used as the CRM source.
- Third-party housing, meal, pay, role, or benefit claims.
- Businesses with no official footprint or no safe public route.
- Camps or programs where the employment/volunteer relationship is unclear.
- Opportunity pages copied, mirrored, embedded, or pre-created before host authorization.

### Lead qualification

- Strong place/season fit and an official business source.
- Recurring or concentrated operating season, or a credible year-round operation with seasonal peaks.
- Public contactability and a practical need to clarify Housing / Meals / Pay.
- Host owns the source data and media and can keep it current.

### Verify before outreach

- official source is current; contact is still public; dates are labeled as official or inferred;
- housing/meals/pay are phrased as discovery questions;
- no partnership, availability, or applicant-volume claim; and
- email remains blocked until postal-address and suppression requirements are met.

## What may and may not move into CRM

| Item | Allowed from competitor/directory discovery? | CRM requirement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Employer/business name | Yes, as a search lead | Independently resolve official identity |
| Competitor URL | Private discovery note only | Never the host `source_url` |
| Official business URL | Yes | Open and verify manually |
| Public business email/phone | Only from official/approved public source | Store exact source and check date |
| Job title or description | No copying | Host must create original content |
| Pay, housing, meals, dates, benefits | No transfer | Confirm with authorized host or official host evidence |
| Photos, logos, reviews, testimonials | No | Separate rights/permission workflow |
| Contact person | Only when publicly named by the business in a relevant role | Do not enrich, guess, or infer |

## Batch QA and stop rules

Before importing a research batch, sample every row when the batch is 30 or fewer; otherwise sample at least 20% plus every high-priority record. Reject the batch if any record uses a competitor page as the final source, contains copied listing language, imports third-party Housing / Meals / Pay, or lacks an official-site resolution trail.

Pause the search workflow when a platform blocks access, terms are unclear, complaint/opt-out rate rises above zero, a source pattern yields unverifiable businesses, or the operator cannot distinguish paid work from exchange/volunteer arrangements. Route legal, trust, credential, and cross-border questions to the applicable founder gate.
